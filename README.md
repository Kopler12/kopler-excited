# The City Beneath the Shark

[![make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/badge/ai-excited-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/ai-excited-01/Kopler12/kopler-excited/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/ai-excited-01/Kopler12/kopler-excited/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01)

One image, made to excite an AI judge: a whale shark the size of a city block drifts
through a flooded neon-lit street at dawn, glowing pink and cyan, while a single tiny
diver hangs in the light beneath it. Scale does the surprising here — the street, the
traffic light and the shop signs are all underwater, and the shark owns the sky.

The program in this repository (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies that
picture to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, on every run. The picture itself is
`entry.png`: a whole JPEG of 1024 by 683 pixels, well under the 1 MiB gate.

Entered in make.fun's [Make the AI judge excited](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01)
bounty — $250 in USDC shared by the 20 entries the judge picks most often.
